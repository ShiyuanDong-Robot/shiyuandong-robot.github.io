I am currently a Ph.D. student at the IT3D Lab at the School of Biomedical Engineering, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, supervised by Prof. Fang Chen, and Hongen Liao. Previously, I was a visiting student with the Research Centre for Opto-Electronic Engineering and Technology, Shenzhen Institute of Advanced Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, guided by Prof. Tiantian Xu, Zhengkun Yi, and Yuming Dong; the State Key Laboratory of Robotics and System, Harbin Institute of Technology, guided by Prof. Wei Dong, and Yongzhuo Gao; and the College of Intelligent Robotics and Advanced Manufacturing, Fudan Univerisity, guided by Prof. Yunlong Zhu and Chenyao Bai.

Recent focus: FBG-based sensors, medical instruments design, and medical robotics sensing technologies.
