About Me

I am currently a Ph.D. student at the Institute of Medical Robotics (IMR), Shanghai Jiao Tong University, supervised by Prof. Fang Chen and Prof. Hongen Liao.

Previously, I was a visiting student with the Research Centre for Opto-Electronic Engineering and Technology, Shenzhen Institute of Advanced Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, guided by Prof. Tiantian Xu, Zhengkun Yi, and Yuming Dong; the State Key Laboratory of Robotics and System, Harbin Institute of Technology, guided by Prof. Wei Dong and Yongzhuo Gao; and the College of Intelligent Robotics and Advanced Manufacturing, Fudan University, guided by Prof. Yunlong Zhu and Chenyao Bai.

Recent focus: Force and shape sensing, navigation, magnetic controlling for medical robotics.
